Side projects

GardenMitra | AI-Powered Gardening

View website

Problem: People starting out with houseplants in Bangalore spend money at nurseries on plants that often die, with no one to tell them why, and have no easy, affordable way to get healthy plants from people who already grow them well.

Who has it: A newcomer wants a few plants at home, buys them from a nursery and gets no care guidance. When a plant dies, they can’t tell whether it was watering, light or a poor plant, so they either stop or buy again.

What I built: A community platform, built with Claude, Lovable and Cursor, where gardeners swap plants locally. AI identifies plants from photos and suggests plants that suit a user’s available space and growing conditions.

Kutcheri Calling

View website

Problem: Carnatic concert information is fragmented across newspapers, individual ticketing sites and performers’ Instagram pages, and unlike other music genres, there is no single event website for it.

Who has it: The admin, who finds it hard to add events manually from different sources such as newspaper cuttings, ticket links and Instagram posts.

What I built: A concert discovery site, built with Claude and Cursor. AI reads event images and pasted text, cleans up the data (performer name, venue, ticket link) and adds the event, which makes the admin’s job easier.
