Side projects

GardenMitra | AI-Powered Gardening

View website

Problem: Plants have become expensive in metro cities. First-time houseplant owners spend money at nurseries on plants that often die, with no guidance on why, and healthy, affordable plants from experienced local growers are hard to find.

Who has it: A newcomer who buys a few plants from a nursery and receives no care guidance. When one dies, they can’t tell whether the cause was watering, light or a weak plant, so they either give up or buy again.

What I built: A community platform, built with Claude and Lovable, where gardeners swap plants with others nearby. AI identifies plants from photos and recommends ones suited to a user’s available space and growing conditions.

Kutcheri Calling

View website

Problem: For a Carnatic music lover, finding out who is performing this week can mean checking newspapers, ticketing sites and Instagram, and still missing a concert.

Who has it: Carnatic music lovers who want to find concerts in their city, and the admin who maintains the listings. For the admin, each event has to be entered by hand from a different source, whether a newspaper cutting, a ticket link or an Instagram post.

What I built: A concert discovery site, built with Lovable. AI reads event posters and pasted text, extracts and cleans up the performer, venue and ticket link, and adds the event, turning a manual chore into a quick review.
